Privacy

Privacy.

What this website collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

CustomPowerLabs is a prototype engineering consultancy based in Hampshire, England. For anything to do with your data, email info@custompowerlabs.com.

Enquiries

When you send the contact form or email us, we receive what you choose to give: your name, your email address, your company if you add it, and your message. We use it to reply and to discuss the work you are asking about. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us.

The form goes to our own server, which Amazon Web Services hosts for us in London. The server stores your enquiry, emails it to us, and sends you a short confirmation. Our email is provided by Namecheap.

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any work that follows.

Project updates

If you register interest in one of our projects, we keep your email address so that we can send you occasional updates about that project. Nothing else is sent to it, and it is never shared or sold.

You join the list only after you click the link in the confirmation email we send. If you never click it, your address is deleted after 30 days. Every update has a link to unsubscribe, and you can also email us to be taken off. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Analytics and cookies

This website sets no cookies unless you accept analytics. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and _ga_LR9Z0N8S60) so that we can count visits and see which pages are used. Google processes that information for us and may do so outside the UK. Our legal basis is your consent.

If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all. Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, and you can change it whenever you like.

Hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security. Fonts and images come from this website itself, so no other company is contacted when you visit unless you accept analytics.

When you send a form, our server briefly holds your IP address in memory to stop the forms being flooded, and stores only a scrambled version of it alongside your message.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email info@custompowerlabs.com and we will sort it out.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
