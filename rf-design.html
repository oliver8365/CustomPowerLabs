How we work

Designed for the test house, not just the bench.

A radio that works on the bench can still fail in the product. The enclosure detunes the antenna, the processor's clock harmonics land in the receive band, or the ground plane is too small for the antenna to work. We design for the finished product from the start: antenna placement and keep-outs, a matching network with room to tune, and a layout that keeps digital noise away from the receiver.

Choosing between a pre-certified module and a chip-down design is one of the first decisions. A module saves most of the radio approval work. A chip-down design costs less per unit and fits smaller products, but needs full radio testing. We will tell you which suits your volumes and timescale.

We lay out with UKCA and CE radio and EMC testing in mind, so the product has the best chance of passing first time at an accredited test house.

Questions

Common questions.

Should we use a radio module or a chip-down design?

A pre-certified module is usually the faster and safer route for low volumes, because much of the radio testing is already done. A chip-down design is cheaper per unit and smaller, which matters at higher volumes, but it needs full radio approval. It depends on your volumes, size and timescale, and we will help you decide.

What frequencies do you work at?

Our designs so far run from 868 MHz to 3.6 GHz, including sub-gigahertz telemetry, 2.4 GHz wireless and a software-defined radio. If your product needs something else, ask us.

Our product has poor wireless range. Can you help?

Often, yes. Poor range usually comes from a short list of causes: an unmatched or detuned antenna, a ground plane that is too small, the enclosure or the user's hand detuning the antenna, or noise from the product's own electronics. We review the design and the link budget to find which.

Can you help with radio approvals?

We design for UKCA and CE radio and EMC requirements and can help you prepare the technical information the test house asks for. The tests themselves are carried out by an accredited lab.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share the details of your product.
