Overview

Our own machine, built the way we build for clients.

Five-axis machining makes complex parts in a single setup, but the machines that do it are usually large and expensive. TALOS‑5 is our answer: a compact five-axis mill designed from the ground up by our team.

Every part of the machine is designed, assembled and checked as a complete digital model before anything is made. It is the same simulation-first process we use on client prototypes.

01

Simulation first

Motion, clearances and assembly are proven in software before metal is cut.

02

Designed in-house

Key motion components are our own designs rather than off-the-shelf compromises.

03

Made to be made

Designed around processes a small workshop can produce and assemble.
