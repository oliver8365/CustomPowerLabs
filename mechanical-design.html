How we work

Simulation first.

A physical prototype is an expensive way to find a mistake. We build the complete product as a digital model first and check motion, clearances, assembly, strength and cooling in software. By the time anything is made, the first build is close to right.

Our own TALOS-5 five-axis mill was designed this way. Every part was designed, assembled and checked as a complete digital model before any metal was cut.

We design in SolidWorks and supply native files, STEP models, STL files for printing and dimensioned drawings, so your team or your manufacturer can pick up where we finish.

Questions

Common questions.

What files will we receive?

Native SolidWorks files, STEP models for anyone using other CAD software, STL files for 3D printing, and dimensioned PDF drawings for machined parts.

Do we need simulation?

Not always. Simulation earns its cost when a part is hard to change once made, when failure is expensive, or when heat or airflow decide whether the product works. For simple brackets and covers, a quick print and test is often faster.

Can you design an enclosure around our existing PCB?

Yes. Send us the board outline or the 3D model and we will design around it. If the board can change too, we can adjust both so they fit together better.
