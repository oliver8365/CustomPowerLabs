About these tools

Quick answers, shown working.

We built these for our own design work and made them public. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and no account is needed.

Each calculator uses published, citable methods, and the page beneath it explains the equations and where they stop being accurate. Use them for first-pass design and sanity checks. For a final design, confirm against your parts' datasheets, your board house's stackup, and measurement.

Every result can be shared: change the values, then use "Copy link to these values" to send the exact case to a colleague.
