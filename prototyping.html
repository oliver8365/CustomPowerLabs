How we work

One team, one price, no gaps.

Most prototypes go wrong at the boundaries: the board does not fit the enclosure, the firmware does not match the hardware, and each supplier blames the next. We design all of it, so those problems are ours to solve before they reach you.

Every project starts with a free discovery call. After it we send a proposal with a fixed scope, timeline and price, and regular reviews with models and simulation results mean nothing is a surprise.

We work with start-ups and established companies across the UK. We are based in Hampshire and work with most clients by call and video, visiting when it helps.

Questions

Common questions.

How much does a prototype cost?

It depends on what the prototype has to do. After a free discovery call we send a fixed price for an agreed scope, so you know the cost before any work starts.

How long does it take?

A simple proof of concept can take weeks; a full engineering prototype with custom electronics, firmware and mechanics takes longer. The proposal sets out the timeline, and we keep you updated at every review.

Do you work with start-ups?

Yes. We work with founders who need a prototype to test an idea or show investors, and with established engineering teams who need extra capacity or skills.

Where are you based?

Hampshire, England. We work with clients anywhere in the UK, mostly by call and video, and visit when it helps.

What happens after the prototype?

We focus on prototypes and the step after: design for manufacture, and the files and documentation a contract manufacturer needs to take the product into production.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share the details of your idea.
